'Life dashboard prototype

One time axis, four lanes

Pick a range; each range sets its own bucket size. Orange bands are breaks of 10 min or more (30 min or more on the 7 and 30 day views); violet bands are gaps of 3 hours or more, which is sleep or away. Local time. Newest data at the right edge.

Prompts come from the transcripts on this machine (my count is higher than metrics.db's 1,977 for 31 days, probably because it includes injected user messages; treat as an estimate). Cost is spread evenly across each session's run (an assumption). Sessions running counts a session in every bucket it overlaps.
