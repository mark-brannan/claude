# Summary

Stub. Design: the hand-off design session (`handoff-workflow-design`), where
this was named as the piece the hot-context nag should ask for in place of a
stopping point, and the first half of a hand-off.

Outline, to be filled in:

- Only what the session agent knows: the original goal, whether it was
  reached, changed course or left open, and its state. The numbers (time,
  decisions, friction, items) are the Stop hook's and are not restated here.
- A few lines. No narrative log, no pickup item, no session launch.
- Ends with one line: where a fresh session would take it next.

Open: whether the model may invoke it (the nag points at it) or it is the
user's only.
