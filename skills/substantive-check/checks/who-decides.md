# Criterion 3 — Who decides

Did you change who holds a decision, or what reaches whom? Authority
and routing: what an agent may do alone, what goes to the user, what a
bot's word counts as, who may merge, close, delete, reverse or waive;
which model or effort holds which rung of the hierarchy; what a guard
lets through on whose label. The standing orders sort decisions by
kind, toil to the agent and judgment to the user, and a change to that
sorting is a change to the governing variables however small the case.

Lineage: 10 CFR 50.59 itself is a rule about who decides, the licensee
alone or with the regulator's prior approval; the screen's whole
purpose is routing. Here the record names a functional hierarchy (the
curia-loop's ledger, 2026-11-04): the user final over all, agent layers
with authority over other agents where it serves a purpose and has
measurable value, with oversight and escalation up the chain.

## What a decision route is here

Any statement of who acts, who approves and who is told: "only the
user merges"; "the fixer resolves when sure"; a `human-ruling` or
`human-click` owner on a card; the three exits of a judgment call
(omit, card, ask); the Needs-ruling card format; which decisions the
agora takes and which the curia; a label that lets a change through a
guard (`mixed-loops-ok`, `merge`); CODEOWNERS; who may push to main;
the model and effort assigned to a role (driver, worker, reviewer,
fixer, scoper); what a bot comment is evidence of; what an agent may
file, label, delete or publish on its own. The list is examples, never
a fence.

## Detectors

- **A decision moved between the user and an agent** — something the
  user decided is now an agent's default, or the reverse; a question
  that reached the user now does not, or now does. `read`, both quoted.
- **A decision moved between agent rungs** — the driver's call becomes
  the worker's, the reviewer's the fixer's; a model or effort assigned
  to a role changes (Opus to Sonnet on a rung, a reviewer's tier). `git`.
- **A bypass owner changed** — who may apply a label, a flag or a
  waiver that lets a change through a guard or a check. `git`.
- **What counts as authority** — a bot's comment, a test's pass, a
  merged commit or a ledger line now counts as a decision where it was
  evidence, or the reverse. `read`.
- **A merge, close, delete, reverse or publish right** — granted,
  removed or widened for any party or automation. `git`.
- **Escalation changed** — the chain a finding climbs (thread, card,
  agora, curia) or the condition that climbs it. `read`.

A route restated in other words, with the same party deciding in every
case, is form.

## Declared looks like

A line under `## Substantive:` naming the file, `substantive (3)`, and
the decision that moved: from whom to whom, in a clause.

## `mu` when

The target names no decider and no route, and is routed by nothing
that changed.
